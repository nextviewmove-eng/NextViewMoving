

Design a modern, professional, trustworthy moving company website for a locally owned moving company based in Lethbridge, Alberta.

The website's primary goal is to generate leads and convert visitors into customers by encouraging them to request a free moving quote or call the company.

The design should feel professional, reliable, strong, local, and modern. It should not look like a generic template.

## Target Customers

The website should appeal to:

* Homeowners
* Renters
* Students
* Families
* Seniors
* People moving apartments or condos
* Businesses moving offices
* Customers moving locally
* Customers moving between cities in Alberta

## Main Call-to-Action

The primary CTA throughout the website should be:

"GET A FREE QUOTE"

Secondary CTA:

"CALL NOW"

Other CTA:

"BOOK YOUR MOVE"

## Website Pages

Create Figma designs for:

1. Home
2. Services
3. About Us
4. Get a Quote
5. Reviews
6. Contact

## Design System

Use a professional moving-company visual identity.

Suggested colors:

* Dark navy/blue as the primary color
* Orange or yellow as an accent
* White backgrounds
* Light gray secondary backgrounds
* Dark charcoal text

Use large, bold headings and clean modern typography.

Use rounded cards, subtle shadows, strong CTA buttons, and plenty of whitespace.

## Photography

Use high-quality realistic photography showing:

* Professional movers
* Moving trucks
* Furniture being carried
* Movers loading boxes
* Residential homes
* Office moves
* Happy customers

Avoid cheesy stock photography whenever possible.

## Responsive Design

Create desktop and mobile versions.

Desktop:

1440px wide.

Mobile:

390px wide.

Make sure:

* Navigation works on mobile
* CTA buttons remain prominent
* Forms are easy to complete
* Text remains readable
* Cards stack vertically
* Images resize properly

## Overall Experience

The website should communicate:

"These movers are professional, trustworthy, careful, local, and easy to contact."

The user should be able to understand what the company does within 5 seconds and find the "Get a Free Quote" button immediately.

Prioritize lead generation, trust, simplicity, and mobile usability.
